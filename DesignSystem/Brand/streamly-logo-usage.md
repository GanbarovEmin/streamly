# Streamly Logo Usage

The approved Streamly logo is a ribbon-style play mark and forward motion symbol with a blue to violet to magenta gradient.

Approved asset:

- `DesignSystem/Assets/Logo/streamly-logo-primary.png`

## Logo Forms

- Primary vertical logo: symbol above wordmark.
- Horizontal lockup: symbol beside wordmark.
- Symbol-only mark: app icon, splash screen, compact navigation, favicon-style usage.
- Monochrome: use only when the gradient mark is not possible or contrast requires it.

## Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the capital `S` in Streamly. Do not place text, controls, or artwork inside this zone.

## Minimum Size

- Horizontal lockup: 120 px minimum width.
- App icon or symbol-only mark: 48 px minimum.
- Do not use smaller sizes where the ribbon geometry becomes unclear.

## Placement

Use the logo on:

- Obsidian, carbon, midnight navy, or deep indigo backgrounds.
- Subtle dark gradients.
- Hero artwork only with a dark overlay behind the logo.
- Splash screens and app icons with enough contrast and breathing room.

## Color

Use the approved gradient:

```css
linear-gradient(135deg, #2563FF 0%, #7B3DFF 48%, #FF2DB2 100%)
```

Do not recreate the gradient manually with alternate stops unless generating a platform-specific asset from the source token.

## Do

- Preserve proportions.
- Use the approved gradient.
- Keep the logo sharp and high contrast.
- Use restrained glow if it improves presence.
- Keep the lockup aligned and balanced.

## Do Not

- Stretch or squeeze the logo.
- Rotate the logo.
- Recolor the mark with unapproved colors.
- Add heavy shadows, bevels, outlines, or effects.
- Put it on busy artwork without an overlay.
- Separate the icon and wordmark in a lockup.
- Use low-contrast placements.
- Place it inside overloaded panels or dashboard chrome.
