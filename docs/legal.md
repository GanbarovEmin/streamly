# Legal

Streamly is a media client. It does not host, provide, sell, bundle or distribute media content.

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for:

- the sources they configure;
- the content they access;
- complying with applicable law;
- respecting content rights and licenses.

## No Bundled Library

Streamly does not ship with a media library, torrents, subtitles, copyrighted posters or copyrighted videos.

## No Circumvention

Streamly does not bypass:

- DRM;
- paywalls;
- captchas;
- account restrictions;
- technical access controls.

## Sources

Sources may be enabled, disabled or configured by the user. Source availability, legality and terms may vary by jurisdiction and provider.
